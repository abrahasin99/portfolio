About me

I didn’t take to the academic culture of learning. School felt like memorising for exams, and I struggled with it. Along the way I fell, learned and moved on, and that stretch taught me more about discipline than any grade did.

Then I found my own interests: literature, law, economics, philosophy, history and politics. Reading in those areas taught me to ask why things are the way they are, and the same habit led me to programming. I started with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, moved on to React and Next.js, and I’m now building out the backend with Node.js and Express.

I enjoy building clean, responsive interfaces and working out how the pieces behind them fit together: APIs, data and server logic. I picked web development for its robustness and for how much it rewards fast, learning-based delivery. One day I’d like to grow into a project manager.
